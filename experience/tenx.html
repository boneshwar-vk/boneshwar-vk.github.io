02 The mapping run

Drive, match, close the loop

Rays sweep and strike walls into a point cloud, ICP registers each scan, and the occupancy grid fills behind the robot. The pose graph accumulates drift until the loop-closure constraint snaps the trajectory straight, and an A* path is planned to the goal.

About this visualisation

The run drawn above: a 2D LiDAR ray-cast against the floor plan, ICP scan matching registering each sweep, and a log-odds occupancy grid of free, occupied and unknown cells filling as the robot drives. A Cartographer-style pose graph of submap nodes and odometry edges accumulates drift, a loop-closure constraint pulls the graph consistent, and A* plans a path through the corridor to the goal.

The first scroll holds the floor plan at rest. The second replays the run: everything the wheelchair's navigation stack did in real time, laid out step by step.
